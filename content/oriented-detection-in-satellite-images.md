**oriented-det** is an open-source PyTorch framework for oriented and rotated object detection in aerial and satellite images.

In Earth Observation, objects often have a clear heading: ships in a harbour, aircraft on an apron, vehicles in a parking lot. Axis-aligned boxes include neighbours and background. Rotated (oriented) boxes follow the object footprint instead.
